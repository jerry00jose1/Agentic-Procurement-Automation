## About the data
Mizan Manufacturing LLC (fictional) buys resins, surfactants, pulp, metals, packaging, chemicals and cartons for its two plants in the UAE, plus maintenance, contract labour and services. FactInvoiceLine holds every supplier invoice line with quantity, unit price, due date and paid date; FactPOLine holds the purchase-order lines with requisition, order and need-by dates, ordered, received and rejected quantities, buyer and approver. Join them on POLineID for the three-way match of order, delivery and invoice. Order and payment status show the latest position in the full data. Amounts are in US dollars (USD).

The data is **fully synthetic** (generated by findi360), so it is safe to use in training, portfolios and public dashboards.

## Data model
- `FactInvoiceLine[SupplierCode]` → `DimSupplier[SupplierCode]` (many-to-one)
- `FactInvoiceLine[ItemCode]` → `DimItem[ItemCode]` (many-to-one)
- `FactInvoiceLine[DateKey]` → `DimDate[DateKey]` (many-to-one)
- `FactInvoiceLine[POLineID]` → `FactPOLine[POLineID]` (many-to-one)
- `FactPOLine[SupplierCode]` → `DimSupplier[SupplierCode]` (many-to-one)
- `FactPOLine[ItemCode]` → `DimItem[ItemCode]` (many-to-one)
- `FactPOLine[DateKey]` → `DimDate[DateKey]` (many-to-one)

Load the CSV files into Power BI, Excel Power Pivot, SQL or pandas and join them on these keys.
