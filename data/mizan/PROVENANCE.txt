Company loaded: Northwind Components (ERPNext, Germany, ledger currency EUR).
The source amounts are USD. They are stored as EUR figures with no exchange-rate conversion,
so order, receipt, and invoice amounts stay comparable to the files.

What the sample actually contains:
- 64 suppliers, 51 items, 910 purchase orders (1,909 lines), 905 supplier invoices (1,870 lines)
- Every purchase order in the file is status Closed. There are no still-open orders.
- 65 lines were received after NeedByDate.
- 20 lines were only partly received.
- 19 lines have a rejected quantity, recorded on the purchase receipt.
- 312 invoice lines differ in price from the purchase order.
- 123 invoice lines have no purchase order. Those invoices are loaded without a PO link.

ERPNext receipt documents need a stock item, so service and overhead items are stock items too.
The file stores first and last receipt dates, not each shipment, so each purchase order gets one
receipt on the latest LastReceiptDate of its lines.
Where a line has a rejected quantity, the purchase-order quantity is ordered plus rejected so
ERPNext can accept the rejected units. The source ordered quantity is in the line description.
Eleven lines have a need-by date, a receipt date, or an invoice date before the order date.
ERPNext will not save that sequence, so those documents use the order date. The original
dates stay in the line description.
